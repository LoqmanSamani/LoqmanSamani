## Loghman Samani

**Computational Biologist** · M.Sc. Computational Biology, University of Stuttgart

Most of my work revolves around generative modeling for proteins. I am particularly interested in how energy-based and diffusion-based models can be used for structure prediction, de novo protein design, and conformational sampling, especially when they are built to respect the physics of biomolecular systems. More broadly, I care about developing methods that offer mechanistic insight into protein folding and conformational dynamics, rather than treating these as black-box prediction tasks.

I am currently looking for PhD or research positions in computational structural biology, machine learning for molecular science, or related areas. Feel free to reach out.

### Research Interests

- **Protein Structure & Design.** Generative approaches to structure prediction, de novo design, and conformational sampling of proteins.
- **Molecular Dynamics.** Physics-informed learning of biomolecular dynamics, with an emphasis on scalable simulation methods.
- **Diffusion & Score-Based Models.** Score matching and diffusion processes applied to generative problems in structural biology.
- **Statistical Physics & ML.** Energy-based models, Boltzmann distributions, and ideas from statistical mechanics that inform deep learning.
- **Gene Regulatory Networks.** Hybrid evolutionary and gradient-based optimization for designing gene networks with spatial structure.

### Selected Projects

[**CppNet**](https://github.com/LoqmanSamani/CppNet) · C++ deep learning library built for performance and transparency. Uses Eigen for tensor operations, OpenMP for CPU parallelism, and CUDA for GPU acceleration.

[**TorchDiff**](https://github.com/LoqmanSamani/TorchDiff) · PyTorch library for building and experimenting with diffusion-based generative models, including DDPM, DDIM, score-based SDEs, latent diffusion, and UnCLIP.

[**GRN-Designer**](https://loqmansamani.github.io/grn-designer/) · Computational framework for automated design of gene regulatory networks with spatial structure. Combines evolutionary and gradient-based optimization to reproduce predefined spatial expression patterns. (Master's Thesis)

[**Deep Learning From Scratch**](https://github.com/LoqmanSamani/deep-learning) · From-scratch implementations of MLPs, CNNs, RNNs with LSTMs, and Transformers in NumPy. Covers optimization methods and regularization with emphasis on mathematical correctness.

[**BioStoch**](https://github.com/LoqmanSamani/biostoch) · Python library for simulating biological systems using both deterministic (ODE solvers) and stochastic (SSA, Tau-Leaping, CLE) formulations.

[**SmartSolve**](https://github.com/LoqmanSamani/smartsolve) · Unified framework for implementing and evaluating classical machine learning models across supervised and unsupervised methods.
